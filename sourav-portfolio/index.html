Multilingual Voice AI Agent (Offline)

0.8 sresponse, dataset-matched

A speech-to-speech assistant that runs locally in Bengali, Hindi and English.

It combines multilingual speech recognition, language identification and neural speech synthesis, with a three-tier answer pipeline: lexical matching, semantic retrieval with ChromaDB, and local LLM inference. Unanswered queries escalate to customer care.

Runs on CPU with ONNX Runtime and was tested fully offline: about 0.8 seconds for dataset-matched queries, 2–3 seconds for generated answers, around 9 GB of RAM.
